Annual plan and free trial

The intended US base price is USD 10 per year. Your store checkout displays the actual regional currency, price and applicable taxes before purchase. Only purchase if you accept that price.

Each verified ILMIGO account can explicitly start one free 72-hour trial. Signing in alone does not start a paid subscription. The app trial requires no payment and does not automatically charge or renew. Reinstalling, signing out or changing a family profile does not reset an existing account's trial. A trial is per verified account, not verified person.

After trial expiry, purchase the annual subscription to continue subscription access. A purchased store subscription renews annually unless cancelled in your store. Cancelling renewal normally retains access until the verified paid expiry; refunds/revocations can change entitlement according to the store.

Access and usage

Subscription builds require internet access to verify membership at lesson entry and for live AI. The trial allows up to three AI turns per UTC day; paid Plus allows up to 20. Text correction and conversation share the same allowance. Failed upstream requests can consume a reserved turn.

Optional speech recognition and playback depend on supported installed device services. AI receives submitted exercise text and conversation history; ILMIGO does not retain audio recordings. AI can make mistakes and does not certify proficiency. Current English/Urdu courses are curriculum drafts; the other catalog languages remain coming soon. Do not purchase expecting unpublished courses.

Restore, cancel and refunds

Restore purchases from the same verified ILMIGO account and store account used to buy. An adult manages family purchases; child profiles cannot start trials or purchase. Removing an ILMIGO account does not cancel an app-store subscription.

Request purchase/refund assistance through the store that billed you. The publisher must publish verified business and support contact details before enabling paid sales; those details are not configured in this beta.
