# ILMIGO · Arabic introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

These are introductory Modern Standard Arabic forms, not a specific spoken dialect. Ana aasif is a masculine speaker form; a feminine speaker says ana aasifa (أنا آسفة). Numbers here are citation forms; agreement changes in sentences.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- مرحبًا — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (marhaban)
- إلى اللقاء — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (ila al-liqaa)
- شكرًا — Thank you / شکریہ (shukran)
- من فضلك — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (min fadlik)

**Reading / drill**

مرحبًا
إلى اللقاء
شكرًا
من فضلك

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → مرحبًا
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → إلى اللقاء
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → شكرًا
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → من فضلك

## Introduce yourself politely

These are introductory Modern Standard Arabic forms, not a specific spoken dialect. Ana aasif is a masculine speaker form; a feminine speaker says ana aasifa (أنا آسفة). Numbers here are citation forms; agreement changes in sentences. Arabic model uses a masculine speaker and addressee; vowels and endings change with gender, for example ismuki for a female addressee. Unvocalized script does not display every short vowel.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- اسمي علي. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (ismii Ali)
- ما اسمك؟ — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (maa ismuka)
- سعدت بلقائك. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (saʿidtu biliqaaʾika)
- أنا أتعلم. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (anaa ataʿallam)

**Reading / drill**

اسمي علي.
ما اسمك؟
سعدت بلقائك.
أنا أتعلم.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → اسمي علي.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → ما اسمك؟
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → سعدت بلقائك.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → أنا أتعلم.
