# ILMIGO · German introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

German Sie is a respectful form of address and is capitalized here. Bitte can mean please or you are welcome depending on context. Standalone eins changes before nouns, for example ein Buch.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Hallo — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Hallo)
- Auf Wiedersehen — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Auf Wiedersehen)
- Danke — Thank you / شکریہ (Danke)
- Bitte — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Bitte)

**Reading / drill**

Hallo
Auf Wiedersehen
Danke
Bitte

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Hallo
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Auf Wiedersehen
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Danke
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Bitte

## Introduce yourself politely

German Sie is a respectful form of address and is capitalized here. Bitte can mean please or you are welcome depending on context. Standalone eins changes before nouns, for example ein Buch. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Ich heiße Ali. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Ich heiße Ali.)
- Wie heißen Sie? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (Wie heißen Sie?)
- Es freut mich, Sie kennenzulernen. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Es freut mich, Sie kennenzulernen.)
- Ich lerne gerade. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Ich lerne gerade.)

**Reading / drill**

Ich heiße Ali.
Wie heißen Sie?
Es freut mich, Sie kennenzulernen.
Ich lerne gerade.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Ich heiße Ali.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → Wie heißen Sie?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Es freut mich, Sie kennenzulernen.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Ich lerne gerade.
