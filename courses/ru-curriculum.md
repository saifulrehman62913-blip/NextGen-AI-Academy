# ILMIGO · Russian introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Russian uses Cyrillic. Zdravstvuyte is respectful or plural. Razreshite proyti asks permission to pass. Numbers here are citation forms; their form and the following noun change with gender and grammatical case.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Здравствуйте — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (zdravstvuyte)
- До свидания — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (do svidaniya)
- Спасибо — Thank you / شکریہ (spasibo)
- Пожалуйста — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (pozhaluysta)

**Reading / drill**

Здравствуйте
До свидания
Спасибо
Пожалуйста

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Здравствуйте
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → До свидания
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Спасибо
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Пожалуйста
