# ILMIGO · Korean introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

These Korean forms use polite speech. Annyeonghi gaseyo is said to someone leaving; someone leaving can say annyeonghi gyeseyo to a person staying. Numbers here are native Korean standalone forms; Sino-Korean numbers serve other purposes.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- 안녕하세요 — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (annyeonghaseyo)
- 안녕히 가세요 — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (annyeonghi gaseyo)
- 감사합니다 — Thank you / شکریہ (gamsahamnida)
- 부탁합니다 — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (butakhamnida)

**Reading / drill**

안녕하세요
안녕히 가세요
감사합니다
부탁합니다

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → 안녕하세요
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → 안녕히 가세요
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → 감사합니다
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → 부탁합니다

## Introduce yourself politely

These Korean forms use polite speech. Annyeonghi gaseyo is said to someone leaving; someone leaving can say annyeonghi gyeseyo to a person staying. Numbers here are native Korean standalone forms; Sino-Korean numbers serve other purposes. Seongham is an honorific word for the other person’s name, not your own name. These introductions use polite speech; the respectful name question is intended for an adult.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- 제 이름은 알리입니다. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (je ireumeun Alliyimnida)
- 성함이 어떻게 되세요? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (seonghami eotteoke doeseyo)
- 만나서 반갑습니다. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (mannaseo bangapseumnida)
- 저는 배우고 있습니다. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (jeoneun baeugo itseumnida)

**Reading / drill**

제 이름은 알리입니다.
성함이 어떻게 되세요?
만나서 반갑습니다.
저는 배우고 있습니다.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → 제 이름은 알리입니다.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → 성함이 어떻게 되세요?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → 만나서 반갑습니다.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → 저는 배우고 있습니다.
