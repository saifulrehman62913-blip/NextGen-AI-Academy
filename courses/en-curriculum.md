## Beginner

### 1. Your first hello / پہلا سلام

Greet someone and ask how they are.

سلام کرنا اور حال پوچھنا سیکھیں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- Hello — سلام (heh-LOH)
- Good morning — صبح بخیر (good MOR-ning)
- How are you? — آپ کیسے ہیں؟ (how ar yoo)
- I am well, thank you. — میں ٹھیک ہوں، شکریہ۔ (eye am wel, thank yoo)

**Pattern / usage**

Use a greeting, a question and a polite reply. In Urdu, aap (آپ) is a respectful form of you.

سلام، حال پوچھنے کا سوال اور شائستہ جواب دہرائیں۔ آپ احترام کا لفظ ہے۔

**Reading**

Hello! Good morning. How are you? I am well, thank you.

**Speaking and writing task**

Write three sentences using today’s phrases. Read them aloud, check each word, and repeat without looking.

آج کے جملوں سے تین جملے لکھیں۔ اونچی آواز میں پڑھیں، الفاظ دیکھیں اور پھر بغیر دیکھے دہرائیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Uses the target phrases accurately
- Pronounces slowly and intelligibly
- Can repeat without reading

**Assessment and answer key**

1. Which phrase is a morning greeting? / صبح سلام کرنے کے لیے کون سا جملہ استعمال ہوتا ہے؟
   Options: Good night | Good morning | Goodbye
   Answer: Good morning

## Elementary

### 5. Make a plan / وقت اور منصوبہ

Ask about time and agree on a simple plan.

وقت پوچھیں اور ایک سادہ منصوبہ طے کریں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- What time is it? — کیا وقت ہوا ہے؟ (What time is it?)
- It is three o’clock. — تین بجے ہیں۔ (It is three o’clock.)
- Are you free tomorrow? — کیا آپ کل فارغ ہیں؟ (Are you free tomorrow?)
- Let’s meet at four. — آئیں چار بجے ملیں۔ (Let’s meet at four.)
- The class starts at nine. — کلاس نو بجے شروع ہوتی ہے۔ (The class starts at nine.)
- Please arrive on time. — براہ کرم وقت پر آئیں۔ (Please arrive on time.)

**Pattern / usage**

Use at with a clock time: at four. Use tomorrow without a preposition. Confirm day, time and place separately.

انگریزی میں گھڑی کے وقت سے پہلے at آتا ہے: at four۔ tomorrow سے پہلے حرفِ جار نہیں آتا۔ دن، وقت اور جگہ الگ واضح کریں۔

**Reading**

Sara and Ali want to practise after class. Their class starts at nine in the morning and finishes at ten. Sara is busy this afternoon, but she is free tomorrow. They agree to meet at four tomorrow in the library. Ali writes the time in his calendar. Before they leave, Sara asks which entrance to use. Ali says they can meet by the main door. They repeat the day, time and place so that both understand the plan.

**Dialogue**

- A: Are you free tomorrow?
- B: Yes. What time shall we meet?
- A: Let’s meet at four in the library.
- B: Tomorrow at four, by the main door.

**Speaking and writing task**

Write a four-line invitation with a day, time and place. Then change one detail and confirm the revised plan aloud.

چار سطروں کی دعوت لکھیں جس میں دن، وقت اور جگہ ہوں۔ پھر ایک بات بدل کر نیا منصوبہ بول کر واضح کریں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. When will Sara and Ali meet? / سارہ اور علی کب ملیں گے؟
   Options: Today at nine. | Tomorrow at ten. | Tomorrow at four.
   Answer: Tomorrow at four.
2. Which preposition goes before a clock time? / انگریزی میں گھڑی کے وقت سے پہلے کون سا لفظ آتا ہے؟
   Options: at | on | in
   Answer: at
3. What should a clear meeting plan include? / ملاقات کے واضح منصوبے میں کیا ہونا چاہیے؟
   Options: Only a name. | Day, time and place. | Only a date.
   Answer: Day, time and place.

### 6. Shop politely / خریداری کی گفتگو

Ask a price, quantity and availability.

قیمت، مقدار اور دستیابی پوچھیں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- How much does this cost? — اس کی قیمت کتنی ہے؟ (How much does this cost?)
- I would like two notebooks. — مجھے دو کاپیاں چاہئیں۔ (I would like two notebooks.)
- Do you have a smaller size? — کیا آپ کے پاس چھوٹا سائز ہے؟ (Do you have a smaller size?)
- That is too expensive for me. — یہ میرے لیے بہت مہنگا ہے۔ (That is too expensive for me.)
- May I have a receipt? — کیا مجھے رسید مل سکتی ہے؟ (May I have a receipt?)
- I will take this one. — میں یہ لے لیتا ہوں۔ (I will take this one.)

**Pattern / usage**

I would like is a polite request. Use how much for a price and how many for a countable quantity.

I would like شائستہ درخواست ہے۔ قیمت کے لیے how much اور گنی جا سکنے والی تعداد کے لیے how many استعمال کریں۔

**Reading**

Hina needs two notebooks for a course. In the shop, she asks the price before choosing a colour. Each notebook costs one hundred rupees, so two cost two hundred rupees. A larger notebook costs more, but Hina does not need it. She chooses two small blue notebooks. At the counter, she checks the total and asks for a receipt. She thanks the shopkeeper before leaving. A clear quantity and a polite question make the purchase easier.

**Dialogue**

- A: How much is one notebook?
- B: One hundred rupees. How many would you like?
- A: Two small notebooks, please.
- B: Here you are. Your total is two hundred rupees.

**Speaking and writing task**

Role-play a purchase of three items. Ask the price and quantity, check the total, and request a receipt. Write the final exchange.

تین چیزوں کی خریداری کی مشق کریں۔ قیمت اور مقدار پوچھیں، کل رقم دیکھیں اور رسید مانگیں۔ آخری گفتگو لکھیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. How much do Hina’s two notebooks cost? / حنا کی دو کاپیوں کی قیمت کتنی ہے؟
   Options: One hundred rupees. | Three hundred rupees. | Two hundred rupees.
   Answer: Two hundred rupees.
2. Which phrase politely requests an item? / کون سا جملہ شائستگی سے چیز مانگتا ہے؟
   Options: I would like two notebooks. | Give it now! | I am tomorrow.
   Answer: I would like two notebooks.
3. What does Hina request at the counter? / حنا کاؤنٹر پر کیا مانگتی ہے؟
   Options: A ticket. | A receipt. | A meeting.
   Answer: A receipt.

### 7. Find your way / راستہ پوچھیں

Ask for directions and repeat the route.

راستہ پوچھیں اور ہدایات دہرائیں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- Excuse me, where is the library? — معاف کیجیے، لائبریری کہاں ہے؟ (Excuse me, where is the library?)
- Go straight ahead. — سیدھا آگے جائیں۔ (Go straight ahead.)
- Turn left at the bank. — بینک کے پاس بائیں مڑیں۔ (Turn left at the bank.)
- It is next to the school. — یہ سکول کے ساتھ ہے۔ (It is next to the school.)
- Is it within walking distance? — کیا وہاں پیدل جا سکتے ہیں؟ (Is it within walking distance?)
- Could you repeat that, please? — کیا آپ دوبارہ بتا سکتے ہیں؟ (Could you repeat that, please?)

**Pattern / usage**

Imperatives give directions: go, turn, stop. Next to means beside; opposite means across from.

ہدایات میں فعل کی بنیادی شکل آتی ہے: go، turn، stop۔ next to کا مطلب ساتھ اور opposite کا مطلب سامنے ہے۔

**Reading**

Omar is new to the neighbourhood. He wants to visit the library, so he asks a person near the bus stop. The person tells him to walk straight ahead and turn left at the bank. The library is next to the school, not opposite the bank. Omar repeats the route to check that he understood it. He learns that the walk takes about five minutes. He thanks the person and follows the directions. A landmark is often easier to remember than a street number.

**Dialogue**

- A: Where is the library?
- B: Go straight, then turn left at the bank.
- A: Left at the bank, then next to the school?
- B: That’s right. It is about five minutes away.

**Speaking and writing task**

Draw a simple route from a bus stop to a library. Give three directions, use one landmark, and ask your partner to repeat them.

بس سٹاپ سے لائبریری تک سادہ نقشہ بنائیں۔ تین ہدایات دیں، ایک نشانی بتائیں اور ساتھی سے ہدایات دہرائیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. Where should Omar turn? / عمر کہاں مڑے؟
   Options: Right at the school. | Behind the bus. | Left at the bank.
   Answer: Left at the bank.
2. Where is the library? / لائبریری کہاں ہے؟
   Options: Next to the school. | Opposite the bank. | Inside the bus stop.
   Answer: Next to the school.
3. Why does Omar repeat the route? / عمر راستہ کیوں دہراتا ہے؟
   Options: To change the destination. | To check his understanding. | To ask the price.
   Answer: To check his understanding.

### 8. Talk about yesterday / گزرے دن کی بات

Describe completed actions in a clear sequence.

مکمل ہو چکے کام ترتیب سے بیان کریں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- Yesterday I visited a friend. — کل میں ایک دوست سے ملا۔ (Yesterday I visited a friend.)
- First, we had lunch. — پہلے ہم نے دوپہر کا کھانا کھایا۔ (First, we had lunch.)
- Then we went for a walk. — پھر ہم سیر کے لیے گئے۔ (Then we went for a walk.)
- We returned before sunset. — ہم سورج غروب ہونے سے پہلے واپس آئے۔ (We returned before sunset.)
- I enjoyed the afternoon. — مجھے دوپہر کا وقت اچھا لگا۔ (I enjoyed the afternoon.)
- What did you do yesterday? — آپ نے کل کیا کیا؟ (What did you do yesterday?)

**Pattern / usage**

Past simple describes finished actions: visited, went, had. Questions use did + base verb: What did you do?

مکمل ماضی کے لیے past simple استعمال کریں: visited، went، had۔ سوال میں did کے بعد فعل کی بنیادی شکل آتی ہے: What did you do?

**Reading**

Yesterday, Bilal visited his friend after finishing his homework. First, they had lunch at home. Then they walked to a nearby park. They talked about a book and watched a football game. They did not buy anything at the park. Before sunset, they returned home. Bilal enjoyed the quiet afternoon because he had time to talk without rushing. Today he tells his class about the visit. He uses first, then and before sunset to make the order of events clear.

**Dialogue**

- A: What did you do yesterday?
- B: I visited a friend. First, we had lunch.
- A: Did you go to the park?
- B: Yes. We returned before sunset.

**Speaking and writing task**

Tell a one-minute story about a past day using first, then and finally. Write five sentences; mark the verbs that show past time.

گزرے ہوئے دن کی ایک منٹ کی کہانی سنائیں۔ پہلے، پھر اور آخر میں استعمال کریں۔ پانچ جملے لکھ کر ماضی کے افعال نشان زد کریں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. What did Bilal do first with his friend? / بلال نے دوست کے ساتھ پہلے کیا کیا؟
   Options: They bought a ball. | They went home. | They had lunch.
   Answer: They had lunch.
2. Which question describes a completed past day? / کون سا سوال گزرے ہوئے دن کے بارے میں ہے؟
   Options: What did you do yesterday? | What will you do tomorrow? | What time is it now?
   Answer: What did you do yesterday?
3. When did they return? / وہ کب واپس آئے؟
   Options: After midnight. | Before sunset. | Next week.
   Answer: Before sunset.

## Intermediate

### 9. Arrange and reschedule / وقت طے کریں اور بدلیں

Explain a scheduling conflict and propose an alternative.

وقت کی رکاوٹ بتائیں اور متبادل پیش کریں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- I would like to arrange an appointment. — میں ملاقات کا وقت طے کرنا چاہتا ہوں۔ (I would like to arrange an appointment.)
- Would Tuesday afternoon suit you? — کیا منگل کی دوپہر آپ کے لیے مناسب ہے؟ (Would Tuesday afternoon suit you?)
- I have a prior commitment. — میری پہلے سے ایک مصروفیت ہے۔ (I have a prior commitment.)
- Could we move it to Wednesday? — کیا ہم اسے بدھ کو رکھ سکتے ہیں؟ (Could we move it to Wednesday?)
- Thank you for being flexible. — وقت بدلنے میں تعاون کا شکریہ۔ (Thank you for being flexible.)
- I will send a confirmation. — میں تصدیقی پیغام بھیج دوں گا۔ (I will send a confirmation.)

**Pattern / usage**

Could we + base verb politely proposes a change. An offer is not confirmed until the other person accepts it.

Could we کے بعد فعل کی بنیادی شکل شائستہ تبدیلی تجویز کرتی ہے۔ دوسرے شخص کی قبولیت سے پہلے ملاقات طے شدہ نہیں ہوتی۔

**Reading**

Nadia arranges a meeting with a course adviser for Tuesday afternoon. Later, she learns that a school event will take place at the same time. She contacts the adviser early, explains the conflict briefly and asks whether Wednesday morning is possible. The adviser offers ten o’clock. Nadia accepts, repeats the new day and time, and promises to send a confirmation. She does not assume that her first suggestion has been accepted. A successful rescheduling request includes the original appointment, a short reason and a workable alternative.

**Dialogue**

- A: Could we move Tuesday’s appointment?
- B: Certainly. Would Wednesday at ten work?
- A: Yes, that works for me.
- B: I’ll update the calendar and confirm by email.

**Speaking and writing task**

Write a rescheduling email with the original appointment, one short reason, two alternatives and a request for confirmation. Read it aloud politely.

وقت بدلنے کی ای میل لکھیں۔ اصل ملاقات، ایک مختصر وجہ، دو متبادل اور تصدیق کی درخواست شامل کریں۔ شائستگی سے پڑھیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. Why does Nadia ask for a change? / نادیہ وقت بدلنے کی درخواست کیوں کرتی ہے؟
   Options: She has no calendar. | The course has ended. | A school event conflicts with the meeting.
   Answer: A school event conflicts with the meeting.
2. Which proposal is polite and specific? / کون سی تجویز شائستہ اور واضح ہے؟
   Options: Could we meet on Wednesday at ten? | Change it sometime. | You must wait.
   Answer: Could we meet on Wednesday at ten?
3. What completes the rescheduling process? / وقت بدلنے کا عمل کیسے مکمل ہوتا ہے؟
   Options: One person suggests a day. | Both people confirm the new time. | The old message is deleted.
   Answer: Both people confirm the new time.

### 10. Give reasons and advice / وجہ اور مشورہ

Offer practical advice without giving an order.

حکم دیے بغیر عملی مشورہ دیں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

   Options: To avoid an unsupported delivery promise. | To ignore the customer. | To cancel all orders.
   Answer: To avoid an unsupported delivery promise.

### 17. Answer an interview question / انٹرویو کا جواب

Give an evidence-based example using situation, action and result.

صورتِ حال، عمل اور نتیجے کے ساتھ حقیقی مثال دیں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- My main responsibility was to coordinate the schedule. — میری بنیادی ذمہ داری اوقات کی ترتیب تھی۔ (My main responsibility was to coordinate the schedule.)
- The challenge was a last-minute change. — چیلنج آخری وقت کی تبدیلی تھا۔ (The challenge was a last-minute change.)
- I clarified the priorities with the team. — میں نے ٹیم کے ساتھ ترجیحات واضح کیں۔ (I clarified the priorities with the team.)
- As a result, we met the revised deadline. — اس کے نتیجے میں ہم نے نئی آخری تاریخ پر کام مکمل کیا۔ (As a result, we met the revised deadline.)
- I would verify the requirements earlier next time. — اگلی بار میں ضروریات کی پہلے تصدیق کروں گا۔ (I would verify the requirements earlier next time.)
- Could you tell me how success is measured in this role? — کیا آپ بتا سکتے ہیں کہ اس عہدے میں کامیابی کیسے جانچی جاتی ہے؟ (Could you tell me how success is measured in this role?)

**Pattern / usage**

Use a specific situation, your responsibility, your actions and an observable result. Separate I from we. Past perfect may show an earlier past event: the team had booked a room before it became unavailable.

واضح صورتِ حال، اپنی ذمہ داری، اقدامات اور نتیجہ دیں۔ I اور we الگ رکھیں۔ past perfect پہلے واقع ہونے والا ماضی بتا سکتا ہے۔

**Reading**

In a fictional interview, Zainab is asked about a time she handled a change. She explains that a training event lost its original room two days before the session. Her responsibility was to coordinate the schedule, not to approve spending. She checked the new room, confirmed accessibility and shared a revised timetable. The session started on time. Zainab distinguishes her own actions from the team’s work and does not invent a percentage improvement. She finishes with a lesson learned: confirm essential arrangements earlier and document a backup. Then she asks how success would be measured in the new role.

**Dialogue**

- A: Tell me about a time you managed a change.
- B: Two days before a workshop, the original room became unavailable.
- A: What did you personally do?
- B: I checked a replacement room and communicated the revised schedule.

**Speaking and writing task**

Prepare a 90-second interview answer using a real study, volunteer or work example. Identify your exact role, one action, a verifiable result and a lesson learned. Do not invent experience.

پڑھائی، رضاکارانہ کام یا ملازمت کی حقیقی مثال سے ۹۰ سیکنڈ کا جواب بنائیں۔ اپنا کردار، ایک عمل، قابلِ تصدیق نتیجہ اور سیکھا ہوا سبق بتائیں۔ تجربہ نہ گھڑیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience
- Uses today’s target expressions
- Provides a clear order and reasons
- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

1. What was Zainab authorised to coordinate? / زینب کی بیان کردہ ذمہ داری کیا تھی؟
   Options: All company spending. | Every employee’s salary. | The schedule.
   Answer: The schedule.
2. Which result is supported by the account? / مثال کے مطابق کون سا نتیجہ ثابت ہے؟
   Options: The session started on time. | Revenue doubled. | Every risk disappeared.
   Answer: The session started on time.
3. What makes the answer credible? / جواب کو قابلِ اعتماد کیا بناتا ہے؟

   Answer: Specific actions and an observable result.

### 18. Lead a useful meeting / مؤثر میٹنگ چلائیں

Set an agenda, clarify a decision and assign an action owner.

ایجنڈا، فیصلہ اور ذمہ دار شخص واضح کریں۔

**Plan:** review 5m, words/patterns 8m, reading/listening 7m, speaking/writing 7m, assessment 3m.

**Target phrases**

- The purpose of this meeting is to agree on the next step. — اس میٹنگ کا مقصد اگلا قدم طے کرنا ہے۔ (The purpose of this meeting is to agree on the next step.)
- Let’s focus on the decision we need today. — آئیں آج کے مطلوبہ فیصلے پر توجہ دیں۔ (Let’s focus on the decision we need today.)
- May I summarise the options? — کیا میں متبادل کا خلاصہ پیش کر سکتا ہوں؟ (May I summarise the options?)
- Who will take responsibility for this action? — اس کام کی ذمہ داری کون لے گا؟ (Who will take responsibility for this action?)
- What is the agreed deadline? — طے شدہ آخری وقت کیا ہے؟ (What is the agreed deadline?)
- I will circulate the action notes. — میں کاموں کے نوٹس سب کو بھیج دوں گا۔ (I will circulate the action notes.)

**Pattern / usage**

Use agreed, proposed and decided precisely. A useful action item contains an action, an owner and a deadline; discussion alone is not a decision.

agreed، proposed اور decided درست استعمال کریں۔ عملی نکتے میں کام، ذمہ دار اور وقت تینوں ہوں۔ بحث فیصلہ نہیں ہے۔

**Reading**

A project meeting has fifteen minutes to decide how to collect learner feedback. The chair states the purpose and presents two options: a short online form or individual calls. The team chooses the form for the first round and keeps calls as a follow-up for unclear responses. Sana will draft the questions by Tuesday, and Asad will test the form on Wednesday. The chair repeats each owner and deadline before closing. The notes distinguish the decision from an idea that was discussed but not adopted. Everyone can see what happens next and who is responsible.

**Dialogue**

- A: Which option are we agreeing to test first?
- B: The short form, with calls for unclear responses.
- A: Who owns the draft, and by when?
- B: Sana will draft it by Tuesday. Asad will test it on Wednesday.

**Speaking and writing task**

Run a three-minute meeting about a study project. Record one decision, two actions with named owners and deadlines, and one unresolved question.

تعلیمی پروجیکٹ پر تین منٹ کی میٹنگ کریں۔ ایک فیصلہ، ذمہ دار افراد اور اوقات کے ساتھ دو کام، اور ایک حل طلب سوال لکھیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**

- Addresses the task and audience

- Distinguishes facts from assumptions
- States an actionable next step where relevant

**Assessment and answer key**

   Options: Calls to everyone first. | No feedback collection. | A short online form.
   Answer: A short online form.
2. Who will test the form? / فارم کون آزمائے گا؟
   Options: Asad on Wednesday. | Sana on Friday. | An unnamed person someday.
   Answer: Asad on Wednesday.

### 19. Negotiate scope and deadlines / کام اور وقت پر بات چیت

Explain constraints and exchange conditional proposals.

حدود بتائیں اور شرط کے ساتھ تجاویز پیش کریں۔

**Target phrases**

- We can meet that date if the scope is reduced. — اگر کام کی حد کم ہو تو ہم اس تاریخ پر مکمل کر سکتے ہیں۔ (We can meet that date if the scope is reduced.)
- Which requirement is essential for the first version? — پہلے ورژن کے لیے کون سی ضرورت لازمی ہے؟ (Which requirement is essential for the first version?)
- We should not promise what we cannot verify. — ہمیں غیر مصدقہ بات کا وعدہ نہیں کرنا چاہیے۔ (We should not promise what we cannot verify.)

**Pattern / usage**

Negotiate a constraint with an if-clause and an explicit trade-off. Scope, timing and acceptance must all be confirmed; a proposal is not an agreed promise.

A client requests a bilingual guide with illustrations by Friday. The writer can finish the text by then, but the illustrations still need approval. The client explains that the Friday event requires the text, while the illustrated version can follow. They agree on the number of pages, the review owner and what counts as acceptance. Any additional section will need a new estimate. The agreement changes the delivery sequence without hiding the unfinished work.

**Dialogue**

- A: Can you deliver everything by Friday?
- B: The text, yes. The illustrations still require approval.
- A: Could we accept the text first?
- B: Yes, if we confirm the page count and review deadline today.

Deliver a structured recommendation and answer a challenge honestly.

منظم سفارش پیش کریں اور اعتراض کا دیانت داری سے جواب دیں۔

**Target phrases**

- My recommendation is to run a limited pilot. — میری سفارش محدود آزمائش کرنا ہے۔ (My recommendation is to run a limited pilot.)
- The main reason is the uncertainty in our estimate. — بنیادی وجہ ہمارے تخمینے میں غیر یقینی صورت ہے۔ (The main reason is the uncertainty in our estimate.)
- The expected benefit must be tested. — متوقع فائدہ آزمانا ضروری ہے۔ (The expected benefit must be tested.)
- The main risk is additional workload. — بنیادی خطرہ کام کا اضافی بوجھ ہے۔ (The main risk is additional workload.)
- I do not have that figure yet. — یہ عدد ابھی میرے پاس نہیں ہے۔ (I do not have that figure yet.)
- I can confirm it after checking the source. — ماخذ دیکھنے کے بعد اس کی تصدیق کر سکتا ہوں۔ (I can confirm it after checking the source.)

**Pattern / usage**

Organise a recommendation as problem → proposal → evidence/uncertainty → risk → decision requested. When a fact is unknown, say so and specify how you will check it.

سفارش کی ترتیب: مسئلہ، تجویز، شواہد یا غیر یقینی بات، خطرہ اور مطلوبہ فیصلہ۔ نامعلوم حقیقت پر تصدیق کا طریقہ بتائیں۔

**Reading**

At the end of a fictional project review, the presenter recommends testing a new feedback process with one class for two weeks. The presentation explains the problem, the proposal, a possible benefit, the workload risk and a review date. A colleague asks for a guaranteed improvement figure. The presenter says that no reliable figure is available yet and proposes measuring response time and completion rates during the pilot. The team will decide whether to expand after reviewing those measures and teacher feedback. The recommendation is clear because it asks for a specific decision and identifies what still needs to be learned.

**Dialogue**

- A: What decision do you need from us?
- B: Approval for a two-week pilot with one class.
- A: Can you guarantee a specific improvement?
- B: Not yet. We will measure results and review them before expanding.

**Speaking and writing task**

آخری عملی مشق: تین منٹ کی سفارش پیش کریں، تقریباً ۱۵۰ الفاظ کا انتظامی خلاصہ لکھیں اور دو مشکل سوالوں کے جواب دیں۔ مقصد، ترتیب، شواہد، احترام اور اگلا قدم جانچیں۔ یہ مشق ہے، پیشہ ورانہ سند نہیں۔

**Self-review (not automatic grading)**
