# ILMIGO · Mandarin Chinese introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

These are Mandarin forms in simplified Chinese characters. Pinyin tone marks matter. Shì and bú shì affirm or deny an identity statement; Mandarin does not use one universal yes/no pair for every kind of question.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- 你好 — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (nǐ hǎo)
- 再见 — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (zài jiàn)
- 谢谢 — Thank you / شکریہ (xiè xie)
- 请 — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (qǐng)

**Reading / drill**

你好
再见
谢谢
请

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → 你好
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → 再见
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → 谢谢
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → 请
