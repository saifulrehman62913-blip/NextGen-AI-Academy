## Greetings and courtesy

Vietnamese uses Latin letters with essential vowel and tone marks. Vâng is a respectful affirmative; dạ is also used in respectful replies. Pronouns and ways of addressing people depend on age and relationship.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Xin chào — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Xin chào)
- Tạm biệt — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Tạm biệt)
- Cảm ơn — Thank you / شکریہ (Cảm ơn)
- Làm ơn — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Làm ơn)

**Reading / drill**

Xin chào
Tạm biệt
Cảm ơn
Làm ơn

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Xin chào
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Tạm biệt
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Cảm ơn
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Làm ơn

## Introduce yourself politely

Vietnamese uses Latin letters with essential vowel and tone marks. Vâng is a respectful affirmative; dạ is also used in respectful replies. Pronouns and ways of addressing people depend on age and relationship. Toi/ban is a limited teaching model. Vietnamese forms of address depend on relationship and age; do not apply ban automatically to every adult.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Tôi tên là Ali. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Tôi tên là Ali.)
- Bạn tên là gì? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (Bạn tên là gì?)
- Rất vui được gặp bạn. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Rất vui được gặp bạn.)
- Tôi đang học. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Tôi đang học.)

**Reading / drill**

Tôi tên là Ali.
Bạn tên là gì?
Rất vui được gặp bạn.
Tôi đang học.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Tôi tên là Ali.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → Bạn tên là gì?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Rất vui được gặp bạn.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Tôi đang học.
