# ILMIGO · Sindhi introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Sindhi is written right to left here. The letters ٻ and ٽ represent sounds different from ordinary ب and ت. The same expression maaf kajo can serve as excuse me or an apology; use context.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- السلام عليڪم — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (assalam alaikum)
- خدا حافظ — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (khuda hafiz)
- مهرباني — Thank you / شکریہ (meharbani)
- مهرباني ڪري — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (meharbani kare)

**Reading / drill**

السلام عليڪم
خدا حافظ
مهرباني
مهرباني ڪري

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → السلام عليڪم
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → خدا حافظ
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → مهرباني
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → مهرباني ڪري
