# ILMIGO · Sindhi introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Sindhi is written right to left here. The letters ٻ and ٽ represent sounds different from ordinary ب and ت. The same expression maaf kajo can serve as excuse me or an apology; use context.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- السلام عليڪم — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (assalam alaikum)
- خدا حافظ — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (khuda hafiz)
- مهرباني — Thank you / شکریہ (meharbani)
- مهرباني ڪري — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (meharbani kare)

**Reading / drill**

السلام عليڪم
خدا حافظ
مهرباني
مهرباني ڪري

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → السلام عليڪم
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → خدا حافظ
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → مهرباني
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → مهرباني ڪري

## Introduce yourself politely

Sindhi is written right to left here. The letters ٻ and ٽ represent sounds different from ordinary ب and ت. The same expression maaf kajo can serve as excuse me or an apology; use context. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- منهنجو نالو علي آهي۔ — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (munhinjo naalo Ali aahe)
- توهان جو نالو ڇا آهي؟ — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (tawhaan jo naalo chhaa aahe)
- توهان سان ملي خوشي ٿي۔ — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (tawhaan saan mili khushi thi)
- مان سکي رهيو آهيان۔ — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (maan sikhi rahiyo aahyaan)

**Reading / drill**

منهنجو نالو علي آهي۔
توهان جو نالو ڇا آهي؟
توهان سان ملي خوشي ٿي۔
مان سکي رهيو آهيان۔

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → منهنجو نالو علي آهي۔
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → توهان جو نالو ڇا آهي؟
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → توهان سان ملي خوشي ٿي۔
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → مان سکي رهيو آهيان۔
