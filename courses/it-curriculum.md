## Greetings and courtesy

Italian Mi scusi is a respectful attention form. Sì with an accent is yes. Uno is a standalone counting form whose form can change with the following noun. Double consonants affect pronunciation.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Buongiorno — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Buongiorno)
- Arrivederci — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Arrivederci)
- Grazie — Thank you / شکریہ (Grazie)
- Per favore — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Per favore)

**Reading / drill**

Buongiorno
Arrivederci
Grazie
Per favore

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Buongiorno
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Arrivederci
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Grazie
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Per favore

## Introduce yourself politely

Italian Mi scusi is a respectful attention form. Sì with an accent is yes. Uno is a standalone counting form whose form can change with the following noun. Double consonants affect pronunciation. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Mi chiamo Ali. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Mi chiamo Ali.)
- Come si chiama? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (Come si chiama?)
- Mi fa piacere conoscerla. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Mi fa piacere conoscerla.)
- Sto imparando. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Sto imparando.)

**Reading / drill**

Mi chiamo Ali.
Come si chiama?
Mi fa piacere conoscerla.
Sto imparando.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Mi chiamo Ali.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → Come si chiama?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Mi fa piacere conoscerla.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Sto imparando.
