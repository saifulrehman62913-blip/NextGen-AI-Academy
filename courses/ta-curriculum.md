# ILMIGO · Tamil introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Tamil reads left to right. Poy varugiren is a parting expression meaning that the speaker is leaving and will return. Mannikkavum can serve as excuse me or sorry. Spoken forms differ from these careful written forms.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- வணக்கம் — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (vanakkam)
- போய் வருகிறேன் — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (poy varugiren)
- நன்றி — Thank you / شکریہ (nandri)
- தயவுசெய்து — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (thayavuseythu)

**Reading / drill**

வணக்கம்
போய் வருகிறேன்
நன்றி
தயவுசெய்து

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → வணக்கம்
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → போய் வருகிறேன்
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → நன்றி
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → தயவுசெய்து

## Introduce yourself politely

Tamil reads left to right. Poy varugiren is a parting expression meaning that the speaker is leaving and will return. Mannikkavum can serve as excuse me or sorry. Spoken forms differ from these careful written forms. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- என் பெயர் அலி. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (en peyar Ali)
- உங்கள் பெயர் என்ன? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (ungal peyar enna)
- உங்களைச் சந்தித்ததில் மகிழ்ச்சி. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (ungalaich sandhiththadhil magizhchi)
- நான் கற்றுக்கொள்கிறேன். — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (naan katrukkolgiren)

**Reading / drill**

என் பெயர் அலி.
உங்கள் பெயர் என்ன?
உங்களைச் சந்தித்ததில் மகிழ்ச்சி.
நான் கற்றுக்கொள்கிறேன்.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → என் பெயர் அலி.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → உங்கள் பெயர் என்ன?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → உங்களைச் சந்தித்ததில் மகிழ்ச்சி.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → நான் கற்றுக்கொள்கிறேன்.
