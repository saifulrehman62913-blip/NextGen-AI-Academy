# ILMIGO · Tamil introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Tamil reads left to right. Poy varugiren is a parting expression meaning that the speaker is leaving and will return. Mannikkavum can serve as excuse me or sorry. Spoken forms differ from these careful written forms.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- வணக்கம் — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (vanakkam)
- போய் வருகிறேன் — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (poy varugiren)
- நன்றி — Thank you / شکریہ (nandri)
- தயவுசெய்து — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (thayavuseythu)

**Reading / drill**

வணக்கம்
போய் வருகிறேன்
நன்றி
தயவுசெய்து

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → வணக்கம்
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → போய் வருகிறேன்
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → நன்றி
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → தயவுசெய்து
