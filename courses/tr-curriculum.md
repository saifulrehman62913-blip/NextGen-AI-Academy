# ILMIGO · Turkish introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Turkish uses a Latin alphabet. Dotless ı differs from i; ş sounds approximately like English sh and ç like ch. Hoşça kal is said to someone staying when you leave; a person staying can reply Güle güle.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Merhaba — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Merhaba)
- Hoşça kal — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Hoşça kal)
- Teşekkür ederim — Thank you / شکریہ (Teşekkür ederim)
- Lütfen — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Lütfen)

**Reading / drill**

Merhaba
Hoşça kal
Teşekkür ederim
Lütfen

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Merhaba
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Hoşça kal
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Teşekkür ederim
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Lütfen
