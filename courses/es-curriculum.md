# ILMIGO · Spanish introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Spanish accent marks are meaningful: sí means yes, while si means if. Disculpe is a respectful attention/apology form. Standalone uno changes form before certain nouns; counting is not the whole number-agreement system.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Hola — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Hola)
- Adiós — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Adiós)
- Gracias — Thank you / شکریہ (Gracias)
- Por favor — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Por favor)

**Reading / drill**

Hola
Adiós
Gracias
Por favor

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Hola
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Adiós
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Gracias
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Por favor

## Introduce yourself politely

Spanish accent marks are meaningful: sí means yes, while si means if. Disculpe is a respectful attention/apology form. Standalone uno changes form before certain nouns; counting is not the whole number-agreement system. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Me llamo Alí. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Me llamo Alí.)
- ¿Cómo se llama usted? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (¿Cómo se llama usted?)
- Me alegra conocerle. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Me alegra conocerle.)
- Estoy aprendiendo. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Estoy aprendiendo.)

**Reading / drill**

Me llamo Alí.
¿Cómo se llama usted?
Me alegra conocerle.
Estoy aprendiendo.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Me llamo Alí.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → ¿Cómo se llama usted?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Me alegra conocerle.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Estoy aprendiendo.
