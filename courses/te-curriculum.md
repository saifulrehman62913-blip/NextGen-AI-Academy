# ILMIGO · Telugu introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Telugu reads left to right. Velli vastaanu is a leaving expression. Kaadu is a negative reply for identification; Telugu has other negative forms for absence or actions. These numbers are standalone counting forms.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- నమస్కారం — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (namaskaaram)
- వెళ్లి వస్తాను — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (velli vastaanu)
- ధన్యవాదాలు — Thank you / شکریہ (dhanyavaadaalu)
- దయచేసి — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (dayachesi)

**Reading / drill**

నమస్కారం
వెళ్లి వస్తాను
ధన్యవాదాలు
దయచేసి

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → నమస్కారం
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → వెళ్లి వస్తాను
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → ధన్యవాదాలు
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → దయచేసి

## Introduce yourself politely

Telugu reads left to right. Velli vastaanu is a leaving expression. Kaadu is a negative reply for identification; Telugu has other negative forms for absence or actions. These numbers are standalone counting forms. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- నా పేరు అలీ. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (naa peru Ali)
- మీ పేరు ఏమిటి? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (mee peru emiti)
- మిమ్మల్ని కలవడం సంతోషంగా ఉంది. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (mimmalni kalavadam santoshangaa undi)
- నేను నేర్చుకుంటున్నాను. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (nenu nerchukuntunnaanu)

**Reading / drill**

నా పేరు అలీ.
మీ పేరు ఏమిటి?
మిమ్మల్ని కలవడం సంతోషంగా ఉంది.
నేను నేర్చుకుంటున్నాను.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → నా పేరు అలీ.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → మీ పేరు ఏమిటి?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → మిమ్మల్ని కలవడం సంతోషంగా ఉంది.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → నేను నేర్చుకుంటున్నాను.
