# ILMIGO · Somali introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Somali uses Latin script with meaningful long vowels and doubled consonants. Iga raalli ahow is an excuse/apology expression. The letters c and x represent consonants unlike their usual English values. These are introductory standalone counting forms.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Salaan — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Salaan)
- Nabad gelyo — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Nabad gelyo)
- Mahadsanid — Thank you / شکریہ (Mahadsanid)
- Fadlan — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Fadlan)

**Reading / drill**

Salaan
Nabad gelyo
Mahadsanid
Fadlan

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Salaan
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Nabad gelyo
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Mahadsanid
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Fadlan

## Introduce yourself politely

Somali uses Latin script with meaningful long vowels and doubled consonants. Iga raalli ahow is an excuse/apology expression. The letters c and x represent consonants unlike their usual English values. These are introductory standalone counting forms. Cali is the Somali spelling of the name Ali. Magacaa is a common short name question. Somali c does not have its usual English sound.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Magacaygu waa Cali. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Magacaygu waa Cali.)
- Magacaa? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (Magacaa?)
- Waan ku faraxsanahay inaan kula kulmo. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Waan ku faraxsanahay inaan kula kulmo.)
- Wax baan baranayaa. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Wax baan baranayaa.)

**Reading / drill**

Magacaygu waa Cali.
Magacaa?
Waan ku faraxsanahay inaan kula kulmo.
Wax baan baranayaa.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Magacaygu waa Cali.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → Magacaa?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Waan ku faraxsanahay inaan kula kulmo.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Wax baan baranayaa.
