# ILMIGO · Portuguese introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Portuguese Obrigado is used by a masculine speaker; a feminine speaker says Obrigada. Com licença politely asks permission to pass. Pronunciation differs between Brazilian and European varieties; nasal vowels matter.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Olá — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Olá)
- Até logo — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Até logo)
- Obrigado — Thank you / شکریہ (Obrigado)
- Por favor — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Por favor)

**Reading / drill**

Olá
Até logo
Obrigado
Por favor

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Olá
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Até logo
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Obrigado
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Por favor
