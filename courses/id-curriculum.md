# ILMIGO · Indonesian introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Indonesian uses Latin script. Tolong often requests help; silakan invites someone to do something. Tidak negates many verbs/adjectives; bukan is used in other negative contexts. Keep these phrases in their taught situations.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Halo — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Halo)
- Sampai jumpa — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Sampai jumpa)
- Terima kasih — Thank you / شکریہ (Terima kasih)
- Tolong — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Tolong)

**Reading / drill**

Halo
Sampai jumpa
Terima kasih
Tolong

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Halo
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Sampai jumpa
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Terima kasih
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Tolong
