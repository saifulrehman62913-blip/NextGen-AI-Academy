# ILMIGO · Indonesian introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Indonesian uses Latin script. Tolong often requests help; silakan invites someone to do something. Tidak negates many verbs/adjectives; bukan is used in other negative contexts. Keep these phrases in their taught situations.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Halo — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Halo)
- Sampai jumpa — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Sampai jumpa)
- Terima kasih — Thank you / شکریہ (Terima kasih)
- Tolong — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Tolong)

**Reading / drill**

Halo
Sampai jumpa
Terima kasih
Tolong

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Halo
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Sampai jumpa
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Terima kasih
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Tolong

## Introduce yourself politely

Indonesian uses Latin script. Tolong often requests help; silakan invites someone to do something. Tidak negates many verbs/adjectives; bukan is used in other negative contexts. Keep these phrases in their taught situations. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Nama saya Ali. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (Nama saya Ali.)
- Siapa nama Anda? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (Siapa nama Anda?)
- Senang bertemu dengan Anda. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (Senang bertemu dengan Anda.)
- Saya sedang belajar. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (Saya sedang belajar.)

**Reading / drill**

Nama saya Ali.
Siapa nama Anda?
Senang bertemu dengan Anda.
Saya sedang belajar.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → Nama saya Ali.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → Siapa nama Anda?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → Senang bertemu dengan Anda.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → Saya sedang belajar.
