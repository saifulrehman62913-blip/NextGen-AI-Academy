Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

## Greetings and courtesy

Japanese uses kana and kanji. Konnichiwa is mainly a daytime greeting. Sayounara often suggests a longer parting; casual short-term goodbyes differ. Onegai shimasu is a polite request expression, not a word-for-word equivalent in every English please sentence.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- こんにちは — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (konnichiwa)
- さようなら — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (sayounara)
- ありがとうございます — Thank you / شکریہ (arigatou gozaimasu)
- お願いします — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (onegai shimasu)

**Reading / drill**

こんにちは
さようなら
ありがとうございます
お願いします

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → こんにちは
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → さようなら
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → ありがとうございます
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → お願いします

## Introduce yourself politely

Japanese uses kana and kanji. Konnichiwa is mainly a daytime greeting. Sayounara often suggests a longer parting; casual short-term goodbyes differ. Onegai shimasu is a polite request expression, not a word-for-word equivalent in every English please sentence. Use polite desu/masu speech in this model. Watashi no namae wa is explicit beginner practice; natural introductions can be shorter. Long vowels and pitch require listening review.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- 私の名前はアリです。 — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (watashi no namae wa Ari desu)
- お名前は何ですか。 — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (onamae wa nan desu ka)
- お会いできてうれしいです。 — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (oai dekite ureshii desu)
- 勉強しています。 — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (benkyou shite imasu)

**Reading / drill**

私の名前はアリです。
お名前は何ですか。
お会いできてうれしいです。
勉強しています。

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → 私の名前はアリです。
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → お名前は何ですか。
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → お会いできてうれしいです。
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → 勉強しています。
