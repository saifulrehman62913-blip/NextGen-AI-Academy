Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Hindi uses Devanagari, read left to right. Suniye is a respectful way to attract attention. The dot in फ़ marks a borrowed f sound, and the nasal mark in हाँ affects pronunciation.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- नमस्ते — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (namaste)
- अलविदा — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (alvida)
- धन्यवाद — Thank you / شکریہ (dhanyavaad)
- कृपया — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (kripaya)

**Reading / drill**

नमस्ते
अलविदा
धन्यवाद
कृपया

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → नमस्ते
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → अलविदा
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → धन्यवाद
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → कृपया

## Introduce yourself politely

Hindi uses Devanagari, read left to right. Suniye is a respectful way to attract attention. The dot in फ़ marks a borrowed f sound, and the nasal mark in हाँ affects pronunciation. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- मेरा नाम अली है। — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (meraa naam Ali hai)
- आपका नाम क्या है? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (aapkaa naam kyaa hai)
- आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई। — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (aapse milkar khushii huii)
- मैं सीख रहा हूँ। — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (main siikh rahaa huun)

**Reading / drill**

मेरा नाम अली है।
आपका नाम क्या है?
आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई।
मैं सीख रहा हूँ।

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → मेरा नाम अली है।
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → आपका नाम क्या है?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई।
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → मैं सीख रहा हूँ।
