# ILMIGO · Persian / Farsi introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Persian reads right to left. These are Iranian Persian introductory forms. Lotfan commonly accompanies a request; bebakhshid can politely attract attention or introduce an apology.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- سلام — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (salaam)
- خداحافظ — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (khodaahaafez)
- ممنون — Thank you / شکریہ (mamnoon)
- لطفاً — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (lotfan)

**Reading / drill**

سلام
خداحافظ
ممنون
لطفاً

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → سلام
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → خداحافظ
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → ممنون
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → لطفاً
