# ILMIGO · Persian / Farsi introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Persian reads right to left. These are Iranian Persian introductory forms. Lotfan commonly accompanies a request; bebakhshid can politely attract attention or introduce an apology.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- سلام — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (salaam)
- خداحافظ — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (khodaahaafez)
- ممنون — Thank you / شکریہ (mamnoon)
- لطفاً — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (lotfan)

**Reading / drill**

سلام
خداحافظ
ممنون
لطفاً

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → سلام
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → خداحافظ
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → ممنون
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → لطفاً

## Introduce yourself politely

Persian reads right to left. These are Iranian Persian introductory forms. Lotfan commonly accompanies a request; bebakhshid can politely attract attention or introduce an apology. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- نام من علی است. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (naam-e man Ali ast)
- نام شما چیست؟ — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (naam-e shomaa chist)
- از آشنایی با شما خوشوقتم. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (az aashnaayi baa shomaa khoshvaghtam)
- من در حال یادگیری هستم. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (man dar haal-e yaadgiri hastam)

**Reading / drill**

نام من علی است.
نام شما چیست؟
از آشنایی با شما خوشوقتم.
من در حال یادگیری هستم.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → نام من علی است.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → نام شما چیست؟
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → از آشنایی با شما خوشوقتم.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → من در حال یادگیری هستم.
