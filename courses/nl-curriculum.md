# ILMIGO · Dutch introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Dutch u is respectful; je is familiar. Alstublieft can mean please or accompany handing something over. Een is the written counting form one; één can distinguish it from the indefinite article in context.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Hallo — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Hallo)
- Tot ziens — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Tot ziens)
- Dank u — Thank you / شکریہ (Dank u)
- Alstublieft — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Alstublieft)

**Reading / drill**

Hallo
Tot ziens
Dank u
Alstublieft

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Hallo
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Tot ziens
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Dank u
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Alstublieft
