# ILMIGO · Gujarati introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Four suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Gujarati reads left to right in its own script. Aavjo is a common parting expression. Respectful requests can use કરશો; do not infer word-by-word English order from a whole expression.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- નમસ્તે — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (namaste)
- આવજો — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (aavjo)
- આભાર — Thank you / شکریہ (aabhaar)
- કૃપા કરીને — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (kripa karine)

**Reading / drill**

નમસ્તે
આવજો
આભાર
કૃપા કરીને

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → નમસ્તે
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → આવજો
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → આભાર
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → કૃપા કરીને

## Introduce yourself politely

Gujarati reads left to right in its own script. Aavjo is a common parting expression. Respectful requests can use કરશો; do not infer word-by-word English order from a whole expression. The model introduces a speaker named Ali. Respectful address is used where shown; gender, register and local usage need qualified review. Forms of learning can mark an ongoing activity or a general habit rather than matching the English tense word for word.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- મારું નામ અલી છે. — My name is Ali. / میرا نام علی ہے۔ (maarun naam Ali chhe)
- તમારું નામ શું છે? — What is your name? / آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟ (tamaarun naam shun chhe)
- તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો. — Nice to meet you. / آپ سے مل کر خوشی ہوئی۔ (tamne maline aanand thayo)
- હું શીખી રહ્યો છું. — I am learning. / میں سیکھ رہا ہوں / رہی ہوں۔ (hun shikhi rahyo chhun)

**Reading / drill**

મારું નામ અલી છે.
તમારું નામ શું છે?
તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો.
હું શીખી રહ્યો છું.

Introduce yourself with your own name in place of Ali. Ask a partner their name using the taught form, then use the meeting and learning expressions. Write a four-line introduction and repeat it without reading. Confirm gender and respectful-address changes with a competent speaker; do not invent word-for-word translations.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: My name is Ali.. → મારું નામ અલી છે.
- Choose the taught expression for: What is your name?. → તમારું નામ શું છે?
- Choose the taught expression for: Nice to meet you.. → તમને મળીને આનંદ થયો.
- Choose the taught expression for: I am learning.. → હું શીખી રહ્યો છું.
