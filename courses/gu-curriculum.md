# ILMIGO · Gujarati introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Gujarati reads left to right in its own script. Aavjo is a common parting expression. Respectful requests can use કરશો; do not infer word-by-word English order from a whole expression.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- નમસ્તે — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (namaste)
- આવજો — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (aavjo)
- આભાર — Thank you / شکریہ (aabhaar)
- કૃપા કરીને — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (kripa karine)

**Reading / drill**

નમસ્તે
આવજો
આભાર
કૃપા કરીને

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → નમસ્તે
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → આવજો
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → આભાર
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → કૃપા કરીને
