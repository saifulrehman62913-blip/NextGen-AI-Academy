# ILMIGO · Polish introductory foundation

Original draft: qualified language and audio review pending. Three suggested 30-minute practice sessions. Not a complete professional course.

Reading, partner drill, writing and four answer-key checks per lesson. Roman aids are approximate.

## Greetings and courtesy

Polish diacritics are essential. Przepraszam serves both excuse me and sorry. Dzień dobry is a daytime greeting. Counting forms change with gender and case in sentences; these are introductory standalone forms.

**Daily plan:** 5m review, 8m words/patterns, 7m read/listen, 7m speak/write, 3m assessment.

- Dzień dobry — Hello / greeting / سلام / ملاقات (Dzień dobry)
- Do widzenia — Goodbye / leaving / رخصت ہونے کا جملہ (Do widzenia)
- Dziękuję — Thank you / شکریہ (Dziękuję)
- Proszę — Please / request / درخواست میں شائستگی (Proszę)

**Reading / drill**

Dzień dobry
Do widzenia
Dziękuję
Proszę

Copy each target expression carefully. Cover the model and write it from memory. Match each expression to its meaning. Practise saying all four with a partner; for counting, take turns in order. Do not treat the drill as a natural conversation.

**Quiz answer key**

- Choose the taught expression for: Hello / greeting. → Dzień dobry
- Choose the taught expression for: Goodbye / leaving. → Do widzenia
- Choose the taught expression for: Thank you. → Dziękuję
- Choose the taught expression for: Please / request. → Proszę
